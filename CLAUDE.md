# badrsi

badrsi is a small language model that reads its own neurons and rewrites its own weights. Much of how it thinks about its neurons comes from Nick Cammarata's essay "Neural Networks Are Already Clean" (Goodfire, 2026). Some examples are the echo mark in the atlas, negative poles, blind maximum in `test_neuron`, and the system prompt in `src/badrsi/mind.py`. The full essay is below. It was fetched from https://already-clean.goodfire.com/essay.md on 2026-10-20.
